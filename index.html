Your runs settle on-chain

RetailRun's reward layer lives on Ronin — the same network as Axie Infinity. Points are earned in the app. What they turn into is yours, in your own wallet, and we can't take it back.

How a season works

01Every completed run and request earns Run Points, logged line by line.

02A season runs 90 days, then closes on a snapshot — no recalculations after.

03Points convert to $RUN at a published rate — you get what you earned, not a slice handed out regardless. A season pool caps the total.

04It lands in the Ronin wallet you verified. The distributor contract sends it — not us.
